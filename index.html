Commercial model

Subscriptions fund access. Families do not pay for core care.

Revenue comes from B2G and NGO subscriptions and CSR-funded deployments. A light freemium layer adds optional family features on top, never gating safety guidance or emergency escalation.

B2G and NGO

Annual subscription

Priced per district or per population covered. Includes the three core agents, all three channels, the verified knowledge base, and a reporting dashboard.

CSR

Funded deployment

A sponsor funds a defined cohort, for example 100 households in a block, with adoption and query metrics reported back per cohort.

Freemium

Family add-ons

Optional extras such as saved family profiles and reminders. Core symptom guidance, emergency escalation, and cited answers stay free.

Terms and conditions

Last updated 10 June 2026. Please read these terms before using MedAI.

⚠ MedAI is not a medical service

MedAI gives general health information only. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace a doctor. In an emergency, call 108. Nothing here is medical advice for your specific case.

1. What MedAI is

MedAI is a multilingual, voice-first health information assistant available over WhatsApp, SMS, an automated phone line, and this website. It shares general, verified health information and helps you decide when to seek care. It is run by an AI system, and it tells you so at the start of each conversation.

2. No doctor and no diagnosis

Using MedAI does not create a doctor-patient relationship. MedAI does not diagnose conditions, prescribe medicine, or interpret medical reports. Always confirm anything important with a qualified clinician. If MedAI and a clinician disagree, follow the clinician.

3. Emergencies

MedAI is not built for emergencies and cannot send help. If someone faints, has a fit, bleeds heavily, has chest pain, or struggles to breathe, call 108 at once. For emotional distress, Tele-MANAS is 14416 and KIRAN is 1800-599-0019.

4. Where the information comes from

MedAI draws its answers from public-health sources: the World Health Organization, the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, and the National Health Portal. These sources update over time, and MedAI may not always reflect the very latest change. We show the source on each answer so you can check it.

5. Who can use it

MedAI is meant for adults aged 18 and over. If you ask about a child or an elderly person, MedAI frames its guidance for you as the caregiver. It does not coach a child directly, and it raises its caution for minors.

6. Using it responsibly

Please use MedAI for genuine health questions. Do not use it to harm others, to spread false health claims, or to seek help with anything illegal. MedAI declines off-topic requests and anything outside health guidance.

7. Accounts and verification

On the website, MedAI verifies your mobile number with a one-time code sent by SMS. You are responsible for the number you provide and for the messages sent from your verified session. Do not verify a number that is not yours.

8. Availability and limits

MedAI may be unavailable at times for maintenance or due to network conditions. Language coverage varies. Hindi and Bengali are the strongest today, and other languages are still being tested. We do not promise that every answer is complete or error-free, which is why every answer is general and points you to care when needed.

9. Liability

To the extent the law allows, MedAI and its partners are not liable for decisions made on the basis of general information from the service. You remain responsible for your own health decisions and for seeking professional care.

10. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as the product grows. When we make a material change, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use MedAI after a change means you accept the updated terms.

Privacy notice

This notice explains what MedAI collects and how it is used. We aim to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

  • Your mobile number, when you verify it on the website or use WhatsApp, SMS, or the phone line.
  • The content of your health questions and MedAI's replies, so we can answer you and send a follow-up.
  • Basic technical information needed to deliver the service across channels.

How we use it

  • To answer your question and, where useful, send a summary or facility details by SMS.
  • To improve the quality, accuracy, and language coverage of the service.
  • To measure how the service is used, in aggregate, for the health programs that fund it.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information to advertisers. We do not use your health questions to target ads. Sharing with a partner program is limited to what is needed to run the service or as the law requires.

Keeping it safe and your choices

We hold your information only as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal duties, then remove it. You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To make a request, write to us through the contact page.
